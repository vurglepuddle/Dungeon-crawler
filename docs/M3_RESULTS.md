# M3a: the lamp and the Bell Porter

This is the requested coherent checkpoint: the room/view fixes and the Porter
are implemented, together with a first improvement to generation and loot.
FIRE/EDGE/FOOD/GLOW encounters, TOSS, the Seal reveal and the hinted secret
are left for M3b. Nothing committed. No changes to project settings, exports,
the autopilot, BRIEF or COLLAB_LOG. The editor's incidental project/scene
normalization was reverted.

## Look here first

Run `tests/pilot/m3_room.txt`: four screenshots from the same room, facing
north/east/south/west. The floor seams and ceiling ribs now give distance;
the lamp extinguishes distant surfaces at 3.6 cells. Rooms are three cells
wide. Items have minimum-size sprites and shadows. Plates are larger; stairs
have recognisable rising treads or a descending black well. Own-cell stairs
and items stay visible at the bottom of the view.

Labels use the actual object's projection, with a pointer. Both the object
and its label accept taps. Labels stack in stable ID order and remain inside
the view; their hit areas sit below every label. Each TAKE marker identifies
one object or a pile on one cell, rather than a neighbouring object's loot.
The strip TAKE still opens the general nearby chooser. Pack-selected PLACE
and RETURN remain usable while their cards are open.

`m3_return.txt` shows the signature sequence: find the clapper, inspect the
painted peaceful Porter, read the clapper's provenance/purpose, RETURN, descend,
take the Seal, then RIDE home. Returning lights its chest and wakes both lift
landings with a warm synthesized chord. The lift joins the entrance and the
deepest floor's entry landing; stairs remain viable. With one floor, it joins
entry and upper landing instead. It works both ways, costs one beat and has
no arrival attack. A blocked destination rejects the action without spending
time. The returned item has one persistent owner and cannot be reclaimed or
duplicated. Late return from the equipped hand is allowed.

`m3_keep.txt` retains the clapper as a HEAVY three-damage club and returns by
stairs. The braced-arm limit still protects the Husk's tell.

Review screenshots under `build/m3_shots/`: `room_*.png`,

## What the bot was actually offered

M2 BELL42 offered the same ordinary items on each floor: **stone, club, coat,
two breads, two tonics, bone, shard and lamp**. It was ten items, not ten stones;
the repeated purposes and identical placement were still a valid criticism.

M3a BELL42 offers:

| Floor | Ordinary offerings | Free on entry | Free on exit | Original offerings left |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | stone, two clubs, coat, bread, tonic, bone, shard, clapper (9) | 8 | 0 | 0 |
| 2 | stone, bread, lamp, coat (4) | 0 | 0 | 3 |

Keys/Seal are excluded; clapper counts. Pickups and imported dropped items
are not new offerings. The greedy policy deliberately makes room for a needed
weight and retains duplicates; this is pressure instrumentation, not a model
of human judgement. Across 64 seeds, floor one offers 8-12, enters with eight
free pockets and exits with zero or one; floor two offers 4-7, enters with
zero or one and exits full. Original loot left ranges 0-3 then 3-7.
Later floors guarantee healing/FOOD, EDGE/GLOW and distinct extra kinds.
Detailed reports are in ignored `build/m3_pressure.json`.

## Art, limits and the next module

The Porter painting was generated with the built-in imagegen tool. The prompt
is in `art_src/m3/prompts.json`, the preserved original in `porter_source.png`,
and the 4x contact in `porter_contact_4x.png`. `build_porter.py` reproduces the
64/40/24-pixel indexed sprites using the spike's shared 32-entry palette,
binary alpha and nearest scaling. Game copies are `assets/art/porter_*.png`.
Stairs, plates, landings and the clapper icon are native code art. The wall,
Husk and Finder painting remain the accepted R1 assets. Python is offline
authoring only. No converter was needed for this checkpoint.

The room view is substantially more readable and the restoration gives the
return trip a remembered place and a tangible payoff. I would call this a
promising small run, not a finished replayable game. The clapper's third
damage point has no extra value against a two-health Husk after a guard, so
the keep choice is currently weaker than its card suggests. More loot kinds
do not solve that by themselves. The next encounter should give that choice
real weight, rather than padding the walk.

Still to build: renewable FIRE/brazier/torch/web; EDGE cord or scored panel;
FOOD and the Ash Hound's two-cell lunge; GLOW and optional Wick Ghost;
explicit TOSS and lane preview; the Seal's return glow/reveal; one hinted
secret. Add each with its own pilot and regressions. No new music here.

Unverified: M3 APK export/install, real phone readability/touch/keyboard,
performance, audio balance, Android pause/force-kill durability and human
enjoyment/duration. Larger sprites and underfoot stairs are deliberate visual
conventions; overlapping piles remain stylised. Painted lift scenery and
dedicated restored Porter poses are still code treatment over one NPC pose.
